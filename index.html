🌊 BlueTrend — SEA Seagrass & Watershed Monitor

🎯 Project Mission

Monitor 21,221 seagrass meadows across Southeast Asia, assess land-to-sea pressures from 14 HydroBASINS watersheds, and deliver early-warning risk alerts for conservation prioritization.

👥 Target Users

Marine Protected Area (MPA) Managers, coastal resource agencies, and marine ecologists who need actionable spatial intelligence to prioritize conservation budgets.

🛰️ Key Earth System Variables & NASA Data Provenance

• SST (MODIS-Aqua, 2003–2024): Sea surface temperature; detects marine heatwave & thermal stress.
• Kd490 (MODIS-Aqua, 2003–2024): Water turbidity; measures light attenuation affecting seagrass photosynthesis.
• Precipitation (GPM IMERG v07, 2003–2024): Rainfall rate (mm/mth); drives river sediment & freshwater plumes.
• CO₂ (NASA AIRS, 2010–2017): Atmospheric CO₂ (ppm); tracks warming & ocean acidification pressure on Blue Carbon.

Verified Sources: All trends are computed directly from NASA Giovanni / GES DISC datasets using Mann-Kendall & Sen's Slope, overlaid on UNEP-WCMC Seagrass & HydroBASINS polygons.
